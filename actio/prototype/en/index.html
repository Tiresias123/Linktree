    Stablecoins: between CSA Staff Notice 21-333 and the federal Act, who decides what a Quebecer or an Ontarian may buy? (FR)

    The federal stablecoin legislation is enacted but not in force; the CSA’s interim regime, meanwhile, still dictates to platforms which dollar-referenced tokens they may offer. Two regimes, two logics, and one question no published text settles, as far as our newsroom has found: which prevails when they conflict. For investors and compliance teams.
